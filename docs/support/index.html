Frequently asked questions

How do I play a sound?

Tap any sound to start it. Tap it again, or use the play button at the bottom of the screen, to pause. Choosing a different sound switches to it right away.

How does the sleep timer work?

Choose 2, 4, 6, 8, or 10 hours above the sounds. When the time is almost up, the sound fades out gently over 30 seconds and stops. Choose Off to play until you stop it.

Will it keep playing when my phone is locked?

Yes. Sounds keep playing in the background and with the screen locked.

There's no sound.

  • Turn up the volume with the side buttons and check the volume slider in the app.
  • Check that no Bluetooth headphones or speakers are connected unexpectedly.
  • If it still doesn't play, close and reopen the app, or restart your iPhone.

The sound stopped on its own.

Check whether the sleep timer is on. Sounds also pause when headphones are unplugged, and pause during a phone call, then resume afterwards.

Does Sleep Sounds need the internet?

No. Every sound is built into the app, so it works fully offline, including in airplane mode.

Where is my data stored?

Sleep Sounds has no accounts and does not collect any personal data. Read the full privacy policy.
