Privacy Policy

Sleep Sounds · Effective October 12, 2026

Sleep Sounds is an app of nature sounds for sleeping, made by LONGEST CHARIOT LLC. This policy covers the Sleep Sounds iPhone app and these web pages.

In short: Sleep Sounds does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What the app uses

All sounds are built into the app and play from your device. Your choices, such as the sound, volume, and sleep timer, last only while the app is running and are never sent to us or anyone else.

Sleep Sounds does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts, or photos, and does not record audio.

No accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking

Sleep Sounds has no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.

Website hosting

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may briefly process standard technical information (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and secure the site. We do not use this information to identify or track visitors. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Children's privacy

Sleep Sounds may be used by children. Because the app does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
